---
name: to-spec
description: Turn a grilled-out, validated understanding into a short written spec. Use after grill-with-doc, before to-tickets.
---

Write a spec covering: what changes, which file(s), what the new behavior
is, what stays the same, and which of `CLAUDE.md`'s rules were checked and
confirmed compatible in the grill-with-doc step.

Keep it short. This is a small service; a spec longer than the change it
describes is a sign the change should be split into smaller pieces, not a
sign the spec needs more detail.
